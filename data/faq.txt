CUSTOMER SUPPORT FAQ

RETURNS
Customers can return eligible products within 30 days of delivery.
Products must be unused and in their original packaging.
Personalized products cannot be returned.

REFUNDS
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after the returned product passes inspection.
The refund is issued to the original payment method.

SHIPPING
Standard shipping usually takes 3-5 business days.
Express shipping usually takes 1-2 business days.
Customers receive a tracking number after their order has been shipped.

ORDER CANCELLATION
Customers can cancel an order before it has been shipped.
Once an order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled and must follow the return process.

SUPPORT
Customer support is available Monday-Friday, 9 AM-6 PM.
